Market Maker Arena

Quote a bid and ask every round. Beat three bots and a wandering price to the biggest P&L.

How this works

Every round, you and three bots each post a bid (the price you'll buy at) and an ask (the price you'll sell at) — without seeing each other's quotes. Two things can trade:

  • Crossed quotes: if the best bid on the board is ≥ the best ask, those two traders trade with each other at the midpoint.
  • The customer: one outside trader shows up every round and always takes the best available price. Sometimes it's informed — it already knows which way the price is about to move, and only trades when that's bad for its counterparty. That's adverse selection: quoting a stale or careless price gets you picked off.

At the end of each round the "true value" is revealed and everyone's position is marked to it, so you can see how much you made from spread vs. how much inventory risk cost or earned you. Bots: Steady Eddie (fixed wide spread, never adapts), Nervous Nick (fixed spread, leans against his own inventory), Sharp Sam (widens when the market's choppy, leans hardest against inventory).
