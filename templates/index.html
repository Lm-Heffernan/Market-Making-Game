Market Maker Arena

Quote a bid and ask every round. Beat 3 bots to the biggest P&L.

How to play
  • Each round, you and 3 bots post a bid (buy price) and ask (sell price).
  • If your bid crosses someone's ask (or vice versa), you trade with them.
  • A customer also trades once per round at the best price on the board — sometimes it already knows which way the price is about to move (adverse selection).
  • At the end of the round, the true price is revealed and everyone's position is marked to it.

Bots: Steady Eddie (fixed wide spread), Nervous Nick (fixed spread, leans against his inventory), Sharp Sam (widens when choppy, leans hardest).
